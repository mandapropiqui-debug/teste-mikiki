## Frequently asked questions
### Where is Mikiki Lifestyle Hotel?
Mikiki is in Barra Grande, in the state of Piauí, Brazil (municipality of Cajueiro da Praia), 200 m from the beach. The address is Rua Travessa Eurico Carvalho Silva, 404, ZIP 64.222-000.

### How do I get to Barra Grande?
Fly to Jericoacoara, Fortaleza or Teresina; there is a direct flight from São Paulo to Jijoca. From there, continue by car, bus or transfer. Our reservations team arranges the transfer on WhatsApp.

### What suites does the hotel have?
There are three categories, all with a queen bed and a private bathroom: Encanto (Luxury Suite, on the ground floor), Refúgio (Super Luxury Suite, on the upper floor, with a private balcony) and Mikiki (Premium Suite, on the upper floor, with a private pool on the balcony).

### How big are the suites and how many people fit?
The double suite is 14.7 m² and sleeps up to 2 people. Encanto and Refúgio also come in a triple version of 18 m², with a queen bed and a single bed, for up to 3 people. The Mikiki Suite is double only.

### How much is a night and what is included?
The price changes with the date and the suite, and already includes breakfast and Wi-Fi. Prices are in Brazilian reais (R$). Pick your dates in the booking calendar to see the rate for each night, or talk to our team on WhatsApp.

### How do I book?
Booking is closed directly with our reservations team on WhatsApp, at +55 86 99942-6066. You choose the suite, the dates and your arrival time, add treats and experiences to the cart and send the message. You pay nothing now: the team confirms availability and arranges payment with you.

### How do I contact the hotel?
Through the reservations team on WhatsApp and phone at +55 86 99942-6066, by email at reservas@mikiki.com.br or on Instagram @mikiki.hotel.

### Does the hotel have a pool?
Yes. The hotel has a pool with floating tray service, and the Mikiki Suite also has a private pool on its balcony.

### Does the hotel have room service or 24-hour service?
All suites have room service, and the butler is available on WhatsApp 24 hours a day.

### How does New Year's Eve work at Mikiki?
The New Year Experience 2027 runs from Dec 30 to Jan 4, with breakfast, a welcome drink, treats in the rooms, gastronomy, drinks at midnight and a music program. Check-out is on Jan 4, with late check-out subject to availability. Special packages apply over the year-end holidays: ask our team for the price.

### Can I do a Day Use at the hotel?
Yes. Day Use costs R$ 150 per person, 100% credited as consumption, from 11 am to 5 pm, with access to the pool and the hotel facilities, subject to availability.

### Does the hotel offer massage and other wellness services?
Yes. Mikiki Wellness offers relaxing massage, reiki, lymphatic drainage, sports massage and cupping therapy, in two packages: Essential (R$ 270) and Balance (R$ 310).

### Can I request decoration for a special occasion?
Yes. There are treats for celebrating: Romantic Welcome (R$ 245), Birthday (R$ 245), Honeymoon (R$ 445) and Cinema Experience (on request). Prices are subject to change and availability, and our team confirms everything on WhatsApp.

### Has the hotel won any awards?
Yes. Mikiki Lifestyle Hotel received the Traveller Review Awards 2025, a Booking.com award, with a 9.5 rating badge.
